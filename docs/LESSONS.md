## Design Decisions

### Why Dark & Minimal?

- Link-in-bio traffic is primarily mobile
- Users arrive with short attention spans
- Dark mode reduces visual noise, lets content breathe
- Minimal design = faster load times = better conversion
- Editorial aesthetic matches artist identity (confident, genre-fluid)

### Why No Navigation Menu (v1)?

- Single page, short scroll depth
- Navigation adds cognitive load for no benefit
- Users can scroll entire site in seconds
- If they need specific content, they'll scroll — not navigate

---

## Patterns to Follow

1. **Content-first hierarchy** — Music links are the conversion point. Everything else supports.
2. **Mobile-first always** — 80%+ traffic is mobile. Design for thumbs.
3. **Token discipline** — No hardcoded values. If it's not in DESIGN_SYSTEM.md, add it first.
4. **Restrained animation** — Motion should be felt, not noticed. Subtle reveals, no flashy effects.
